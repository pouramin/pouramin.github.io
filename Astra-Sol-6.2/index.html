02 / GENERATION GAP

Astra 6 vs Astra 6.2

A separate look at the TunnelLab motion-graphics outputs from two generations.

What the files show

Astra 6's Premium Intro is a 12-second, 1080p, 60 fps render with an editorial, flat optical identity. Astra 6.2's TunnelLab Intro is also 12 seconds at 1080p, but 30 fps and built around a different ring/tunnel composition. An attractive output alone does not establish that an older model is generally better: the two runs may differ in effort and production approach.

Scores are a guide, not a benchmark

02

Visual + technical criteria

Ratings consider instruction adherence, geometry, continuity, visual polish and, where source is available, technical implementation.

03

Limits of the evidence

The supplied archive contains rendered outputs and some source code. Build time, retry counts, and equal tool budgets were not independently verified. A rendered motion sequence is not a validated physical simulation.

These experiments assess rendered visual artifacts made using code and rendering tools, not direct text-to-video generation. Ratings are editorial and specific to these outputs.
